MOJO Drive MVP 0.10
===================

This build is based directly on the 0.9 field log MOJODrive_Trip_20260924_210603.csv.

Primary fixes
-------------
1. LOCATION and SPEED validity are now independent.
   - Good GPS coordinates are accepted even when Android reports POOR_SPEED_ACCURACY.
   - Camera matching runs on every good location fix.
   - Bad speed measurements do not contaminate the speed filter.
   - A weak position-derived speed estimate keeps timing usable until good GNSS speed returns.

2. Long speed gaps now hard-resync the fusion filter.
   - Old median samples are cleared after a gap.
   - First good GNSS speed after a long gap resets fusion instead of slowly dragging a stale speed estimate.

3. GPS callback watchdog + GNSS diagnostics.
   - If GPS callbacks stall, the service re-registers GPS updates with a cooldown.
   - GNSS satellite count / used-in-fix count are logged.
   - SENSOR_HEALTH now includes callback age, location age, speed age and watchdog count.

4. Confirmed-camera GPS bridge expanded to 25 seconds.
   - Only already-CONFIRMED cameras are bridged.
   - No new camera is confirmed from dead reckoning.
   - If GPS comes back after the camera is behind the car, the app logs the miss and DOES NOT beep late.

5. Curve-aware hard geometry replaces the old straight-tangent hard cross-track gate.
   - curveCross = min(vehicle-path cross, camera-road tangent cross).
   - Severe curveCross is still a HARD reject and score cannot rescue it.
   - Limits tighten as the camera gets closer.

6. Direction is now a hard gate, not just a score contribution.
   - Far cameras require strong forward alignment.
   - Reliable one-way road bearing uses DIRECTED angle, not only undirected road-axis angle.
   - This rejects opposite-direction records which could previously look geometrically valid.

7. Distance-dependent confidence remains, but cannot override hard geometry.
   - Far cameras need more repeated hits.
   - Near, strongly approaching cameras can still fast-confirm.

8. Guaranteed first camera alert.
   - Global arbitration can no longer suppress a newly warned camera for tens of seconds.
   - Every physical camera cluster keeps a pending first alert until it is actually output.
   - Arbitration still controls repeat pulses.

9. Duplicate clustering improved.
   - Base clustering remains conservative.
   - Extended 110 m clustering is allowed only when type, road direction/axis, speed-limit metadata and road name do not conflict.
   - Opposite reliable one-way directions are NOT merged.

10. Recovery after long GPS gaps no longer creates fake huge approach deltas.
    - Tracking hit history is reset after location recovery.
    - Confirmed state remains sticky but transient approach/away counters are reset.

11. Road-impact stale-location bug fixed.
    - Fresh location: ROAD_IMPACT_CANDIDATE with coordinates.
    - Stale location: ROAD_IMPACT_UNLOCATED with location fields blank.

12. Background robustness.
    - Foreground service keeps stopWithTask=false.
    - onTaskRemoved requests service continuation when a trip is active.
    - UI includes Battery Optimization settings shortcut for field testing.

Logging
-------
Raw ACCEL/GYRO rows remain disabled.
New useful fields/events include:
- gps_age_ms (valid LOCATION age)
- gps_speed_age_ms
- gps_callback_age_ms
- derived_speed_kmh
- gnss_satellites / gnss_used_in_fix
- camera_curve_cross_m
- camera_direction_delta_deg
- camera_hard_geometry_ok
- GPS_LOCATION_ONLY
- GPS_LOCATION_REJECTED
- GPS_WATCHDOG_REREGISTER
- GPS_LOCATION_RECOVERED
- CAMERA_MISSED_DURING_GPS_GAP
- CAMERA_PREDICTED_PASS_DURING_GPS_GAP
- ALERT_OUTPUT first_alert=true/false
- ROAD_IMPACT_UNLOCATED

Important field-test setup
--------------------------
Before the drive, open Battery Optimization settings from the app and set MOJO Drive to unrestricted / not optimized if the phone offers that option. This matters especially on aggressive OEM Android builds.
