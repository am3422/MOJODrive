MOJO Drive MVP 0.9
==================

Changes driven by the real 0.8 field logs:

1. Severe cross-track is now a HARD reject and can never be rescued by score.
   The hard corridor expands with distance to tolerate curved roads, but remains capped.

2. Confidence is distance-dependent.
   Far cameras require stronger geometry and more repeated hits.
   Near cameras with strong approach history can fast-confirm.

3. Duplicate DB cameras are clustered by physical proximity + direction compatibility + type.
   One physical cluster produces one tracking state and one alert.

4. Confirmed/Lost/Reconfirmed thrashing is removed.
   State flow is TRACKING -> CONFIRMED -> PASSED/ABANDONED.
   A confirmed camera never falls back to TRACKING during the same encounter.

5. Global alert arbitration:
   all camera requests are collected, but only the highest-priority camera may beep/vibrate per pulse.

6. Raw ACCEL/GYRO CSV rows are removed.
   Sensors are still processed internally up to about 50 Hz.
   Road impacts are still logged, and SENSOR_HEALTH is written only every 10 seconds.

7. Earlier confirmation:
   strong close candidates with established approach history can fast-track,
   reducing late warnings such as camera 14314.

8. Short GPS dropout bridge:
   already-confirmed cameras continue warning for roughly 1.5–7 s of GPS loss
   using fused speed. No new camera can confirm during the bridge.
