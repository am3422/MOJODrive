MOJO Drive MVP 0.8
==================

This build fixes the camera-engine regression found in the 2026-09-24 road test.

Root cause found in 0.7
-----------------------
0.7 used camera roadBearing as if it were a guaranteed forward arrow. In the real logs,
several physical camera passes had excellent cross-track distance but roadBearing was reversed
relative to travel, so the engine calculated "along < 0" and classified real cameras as "not ahead".
That prevented every CAMERA_MATCH_CONFIRMED and therefore every camera ALERT_OUTPUT.

0.8 camera matching
-------------------
1. roadBearing is now an UNDIRECTED ROAD AXIS.
   - rb and rb+180 are equivalent.
   - It is used for same-road cross-track and road-axis alignment only.
   - It can no longer flip a real camera behind the vehicle by itself.

2. Ahead/behind is now determined from actual GPS travel bearing.
   - bearing from phone position to camera
   - forward delta versus phone/GPS bearing
   - forward projection is independent of DB direction metadata.

3. Distance trend is independent evidence.
   - Each camera tracks whether straight-line distance is shrinking or growing.
   - A real approach can confirm a camera even if DB direction metadata is imperfect.

4. Multi-camera independent states remain.
   - One candidate cannot replace/drop another real camera.

5. Better cross-track compromise from actual logs.
   - 0.7 max ~38 m was too strict.
   - Real road-test candidates showed plausible real passes around ~50–68 m.
   - Confirm corridor is now dynamic ~52–70 m depending on GPS accuracy.
   - The known false parallel-road case at ~80–120 m remains outside the allowed corridor.

6. Confirmation now uses a score + repeated evidence.
   - road-axis cross-track
   - road-axis heading alignment
   - actual forward direction
   - actual approach trend
   - GPS accuracy
   - two consecutive confirmation hits

7. Robust passed detection.
   - Uses actual vehicle bearing + distance growing after closest approach.
   - Does not trust DB roadBearing direction for pass detection.

8. Earlier warnings retained/improved.
   - 23–31 s lead time
   - minimum 420 m
   - maximum 1250 m
   - extra braking distance if a trusted camera limit exists and current speed is above it
   - +90 m threshold margin to avoid edge misses

9. Complete camera audit trail.
   - CAMERA_MATCH_DIAG
   - CAMERA_MATCH_CONFIRMED
   - CAMERA_WARNING
   - CAMERA_URGENT
   - ALERT_OUTPUT
   - CAMERA_NEAR_PASS_AUDIT
   - CAMERA_PASSED
   - CAMERA_MISSED_DIAGNOSTIC

10. ALERT_OUTPUT now logs:
    - tone_start_ok
    - vibrator_present
    - vibration_dispatched
    - alarm_volume
    - alarm_max
    - alert level
    - camera position + vehicle position
    - camera geometry / score

11. Fresh DB copy
    - copies bundled DB into shiraz_cameras_v08.sqlite after app upgrade.

12. Persistent trip logging + IMU road-impact logging are preserved.
