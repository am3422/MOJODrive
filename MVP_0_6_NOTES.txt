MOJO Drive MVP 0.6 patch
========================

Main changes:
1) Persistent live trip logging
   - CSV is created in Downloads/MOJODrive immediately at START.
   - Internal journal is written continuously.
   - Public CSV is flushed roughly once per second.
   - If the service/process is killed, trip_active remains true.
   - The next service start recovers the same trip, repairs the public CSV from the internal journal,
     and adds RECOVERED_AFTER_RESTART.
   - User STOP now explicitly finalizes the trip.

2) Audible adaptive camera alert
   - Uses STREAM_ALARM, because STREAM_NOTIFICATION was inaudible on the test phone.
   - Three ToneGenerator levels: 55 / 80 / 100.
   - Alert cadence increases: ~3.5 s -> 2.0 s -> 1.0 s -> 0.5 s.
   - Vibration grows with alert level.
   - Does not modify Android system volume.

3) Speed-aware early warning
   - Base lead time 18–26 seconds based on vehicle speed.
   - Warning distance is clamped to 300–1200 m.
   - If a trusted camera speed limit exists and vehicle speed is over it, the algorithm estimates
     comfortable deceleration distance (~1.8 m/s²) + 4 s reaction/decision margin + 60 m buffer.
   - A tight <=55 m lateral road corridor is required before audible alerts, reducing parallel-road false warnings.

4) Road surface intelligence groundwork
   - BUMP_CANDIDATE renamed to ROAD_IMPACT_CANDIDATE.
   - Adds severity: light / medium / strong / severe.

Safety:
The warning system is intended to help the driver comply with posted limits, not to encourage speeding.
