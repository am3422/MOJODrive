MOJO Drive MVP 0.15 — Guarded Explicit Negative-Route Veto
==========================================================

WHY THIS VERSION EXISTS
-----------------------
The 0.14 field trip on 2026-09-28 exposed one concrete false warning at the end of the
route: camera 14550 existed on the opposite carriageway/level while the vehicle remained
on the hill route. The unchanged core matcher initially rejected it correctly, but after
the vehicle direction changed the 2D geometry later considered the camera ahead and emitted
a first warning.

0.15 fixes only that class of error in the narrowest reversible way supported by our current
field evidence. It does NOT retune camera thresholds, GPS, speed fusion, direction gates,
clustering, warning distance, alert cadence, overspeed, or the previously validated positive
Road Geometry behavior.

WHAT IS STILL LOCKED
--------------------
The field-validated v0.12 camera/location/speed engine remains the base decision engine.
The baseline guard still protects 29 functions and 22 critical constants.

Generic Road Geometry remains diagnostic/shadow-only:
- support cannot create an alert
- weak_support cannot create an alert
- conflict cannot suppress an alert
- outside_coverage cannot suppress an alert
- uncertain cannot suppress an alert

THE ONLY NEW LIVE INFLUENCE
---------------------------
An explicit, human-confirmed NEGATIVE route may veto the FIRST alert for the matching
camera only when all of these are true:
1. the negative route is explicitly stored in exclusion_segments;
2. the current camera ID/type matches that negative route;
3. the vehicle is within 85 m of the labeled route polyline;
4. vehicle heading is within 60 degrees of that labeled route direction;
5. no already-proven positive approach is currently support/weak_support;
6. this is the first alert (state.warned == false);
7. this is a live-location decision, not a GPS bridge.

If those conditions match, the event is logged as:
  CAMERA_NEGATIVE_ROUTE_SUPPRESSED

The camera remains trackable. If the vehicle later leaves the negative route and genuinely
approaches the real camera, the normal engine can still alert. Once an alert has already
started, Road Geometry is never allowed to mute it.
